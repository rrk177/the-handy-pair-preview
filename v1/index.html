COMMON QUESTIONS

Straight answers

Q1Do I need to be home during the clean?+

No. Most clients give us secure entry instructions once and never think about it again. It's the same two of us every visit, and you get a note when we're done.

Q2Is the quote really fixed?+

Yes. The written price covers labour and supplies for the scope you approved. If something falls outside it, we quote that separately and wait for your go-ahead before touching it.

Q3Do you bring your own supplies?+

Always: equipment, cloths, and cleaning products included, so you don't have to worry about a thing. Prefer we use your own products for allergy or surface reasons? Just tell us.

Q4What if I'm not happy with something?+

Point it out at the walk-through and we redo it on the spot. Miss it until later? Call within 24 hours and we come back, no charge.

Q5Are you insured if something breaks?+

We carry $2M liability coverage and are bonded against loss. It rarely comes up, but it's there, in writing, before we start.
